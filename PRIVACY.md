# MinistryBase Privacy Policy

Operated by 238 Apps (James Mitchell). Contact: privacy@238apps.com

This policy covers the MinistryBase Windows app. In short: **your ministry content stays on your computer, and we do not collect it.**

## The app stores your data on your PC

MinistryBase keeps your data in files on your own computer, in the folder `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MinistryBase` (usually `C:\Users\<your name>\AppData\Local\MinistryBase`). This includes your sermons, lessons, articles, attached files, calendar items, and any member information you enter. The developer does not receive, collect, or sell this content.

## No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or usage tracking, and it does not check for updates on its own. It does not send your sermons, calendar, members, or any other content to the developer or to any server. The Bible text used by the app is built into the installation and is not downloaded.

## Things that open only when you ask

A few buttons hand off to other programs on your computer, for example Send Feedback opens your email program, and links such as a member's website or the optional donation link in the About window open in your web browser. Nothing is sent until you choose to send it.

## Optional online features (off by default)

Settings includes an "Online Features" page for features that may come in the future. It is **off by default**. In this version, if you turn it on and enter a cloud project address, the app only checks whether that address can be reached. You are never required to use it, and everything works without it.

## Sensitive information is your responsibility

Member information and notes can be sensitive pastoral information. You are responsible for what you enter and who can access your computer. We recommend turning on Windows device encryption (such as BitLocker), using a password on your Windows account, and keeping your own backups.

## Backups

Each time data is saved, MinistryBase keeps a dated backup copy in the same data folder, retaining the 10 most recent for each data file. You can also export a full backup zip file from Settings, Backup and Restore, and save it wherever you like.

## Removing your data

Because your data lives on your PC, you control how long it is kept. Uninstalling the app does not delete your data. To remove it completely, uninstall the app and then delete the `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MinistryBase` folder.

## The website and waitlist

The waitlist at ministrybase.app is separate from the app. If you join, we collect your email address only to tell you when MinistryBase launches. We do not sell or share it. To have it removed, email privacy@238apps.com.

## Children

MinistryBase is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be in this repository.
